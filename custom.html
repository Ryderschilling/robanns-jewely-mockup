Meet the designer

Taylor Kerchman.

Taylor joined the family firm in 2002 and found her niche designing custom pieces for clients who want an experience that is special to them.

She works directly with you to customize, design and create. Diamonds, sapphires, rubies, emeralds and most all gemstones. And with Roger, a GIA certified gemologist, beside her, every stone is the right one.

After decades in the industry, the family has studied the trends, researched the metals and jewels, and crafted hundreds of custom pieces for happy clients.

How it works

From idea to heirloom.

01

Consultation

Sit down with our team in the showroom. Bring photos, sketches, or the piece you want to reimagine. We listen first.

02

Stones & metal

Choose your center stone with a GIA certified gemologist, then your metal: gold, silver or platinum.

03

Design

Your setting, your proportions, your details. Nothing leaves the drawing stage until it feels exactly right.

04

Crafted & revealed

Made by hand to be cherished and passed down through your family for generations.
