Family owned since 1975

Rob plus Ann.

Robann's Jewelers was founded in 1975 by Robert Kerchman, his wife Ann, and their son Roger. The name came from combining their names to signify unity. It still does.

Five decades in the desert

From Palm Springs to El Paseo.

The original store, nestled in the core of downtown Palm Springs, became a local icon for 30 years. In 2005 the family moved to The River in Rancho Mirage, and ten years later to upscale El Paseo in Palm Desert, where they could serve locals and visitors alike.

Roger took over leading the company in 2012 after the passing of his father, Robert. Today Roger and Taylor carry the family standard forward, one client at a time.

  1. 1975Robert, Ann and Roger Kerchman open Robann's in downtown Palm Springs.
  2. 1980Roger attends the Gemological Institute of America (GIA) and becomes a certified gemologist, trained to appraise, evaluate and identify diamonds and gemstones.
  3. 2002Taylor joins the firm and finds her niche designing custom pieces.
  4. 2005After 30 years in Palm Springs, Robann's relocates to The River in Rancho Mirage.
  5. 2012Roger takes over leading the company.
  6. 2015Ten years after The River, the family moves to El Paseo in Palm Desert.
  7. TodayFifty years of fine jewelry, custom design and on-site repair for the Coachella Valley.
