Our Story

A desert legacy.

  1. 1975Robert, Ann and their son Roger open Robann's in downtown Palm Springs. It becomes a local icon for 30 years.
  2. 1980Roger attends the Gemological Institute of America and becomes a certified gemologist.
  3. 2002Taylor joins the family firm and finds her calling in custom design.
  4. 2005Robann's moves to The River in Rancho Mirage.
  5. 2012Roger takes the lead, carrying his father's standard forward.
  6. TodayHome on El Paseo in Palm Desert, serving locals and visitors alike.
